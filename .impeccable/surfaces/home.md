## Scroll-generated thought experiment

User requested a new tab appearing further down the page and a memorable thought. After the project showcase, a browser-shaped section opens question, experiment and takeaway tabs as scrolling progresses. The visitor can remove network noise with a clarity slider; the final prompt is “Simple is engineered” and asks what in their own day could be simpler. This is an illustration, not measured project data. A persistent route and moving signal preserve the destination while clutter disappears. Existing white/black/light-blue direction and font system are inherited.

Desktop uses a 230vh scroll stage and a sticky frame. Mobile (760px or narrower), viewports 800px tall or shorter, reduced motion and manually disabled motion use ordinary document flow with all tabs available. Tabs support keyboard arrows, Home and End; scrolling never changes the active panel while a control inside has focus. SVG motion pauses outside the viewport and in background tabs.

## Hero formation refinement

User requested slower, more memorable hero motion. The former floating mark/brackets are replaced by a single 19-second canvas sequence: drifting idea fragments (0–3.5s), curved convergence (3.5–10.5s), assembly into the exact supplied symbol (10.5–15s), and a final blue pulse into the code panel (15–19s). The finished mark remains still until replay. Main headline reveals last 1.8 seconds and never gate navigation or content. Four captions follow the sequence without live-region announcements. Offscreen/background playback pauses; reduced motion and the persistent motion switch show the final static mark. Original fonts, white field, accent palette and remaining page structure are preserved.
